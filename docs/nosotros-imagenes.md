# Fotos conceptuales de Nosotros

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, sin CLI ni API externa propia.
No representan comercios reales. Las versiones finales conservan la composición
original y están comprimidas como WebP. Los originales generados se conservan.

- Interior: `public/nosotros/interior.webp`
- Fachada: `public/nosotros/fachada.webp`
- Detalle: `public/nosotros/detalle.webp`

## Prompt del interior

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for the fictional streetwear clothing brand norte. About page. Primary request: photorealistic interior of a fictional contemporary Argentine streetwear boutique, exposed concrete, black steel clothing racks with oversized hoodies and tees, asphalt gray floor, warm neutral overhead light with restrained deep red accent lighting, a red bench, thoughtful spacious merchandise display, no people. Eye level architectural editorial photo, wide landscape composition 3:2, realistic textile textures, stylish but plausible shop, no CGI look. No text, no logos, no watermark. This is an explicitly fictional concept store visual.

## Prompt de la fachada

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide 3:2 architectural editorial photograph of the exterior of a fictional Argentine streetwear concept boutique at blue hour. Black steel facade, large clear storefront windows reveal concrete interior with black clothing rails, red architectural accent light and a red bench, oversized hoodies and neutral tees. Plausible Buenos Aires urban sidewalk, refined weathered building, wet pavement reflecting red light, restrained atmosphere, no people, no cars prominent. Photorealistic, natural texture, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is explicitly a fictional concept store for norte.'s website.

## Prompt del detalle

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide 3:2 editorial close-up photograph inside a fictional streetwear concept boutique: neatly folded heavyweight black, charcoal and off-white oversized tees on a rough concrete display table, subtle deep red wall behind, black metal clothing rails slightly out of focus, red fabric detail, daylight from storefront window. Calm and tactile, real cotton textures and seams, high-end independent fashion editorial, no people, no branding, no text, no watermark. Consistent concrete and black steel store setting, photorealistic rather than CGI.
